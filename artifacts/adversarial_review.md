# Independent adversarial review — skeptical subagent

## Review boundary

This review was produced by a separate skeptical subagent/context given only
the fictional brief, evidence graph, options, and draft strategy. The strategy
generation conversation and earlier drafting history were not supplied. This
is an independent critique of synthetic demonstration artifacts, not market
research.

## Material challenges

1. **The funnel is confounded.** DEMO-FACT-01 establishes only the fictional
   18% and 11% values, not a statistically actionable trend. Cohort mix,
   seasonality, event definitions, and uncertainty are absent. Describe the
   difference as 7 percentage points, not a causal decline.
2. **Option C bundled two mechanisms.** Combining family permission clarity
   with storage-value messaging would make any effect uninterpretable. Isolate
   permission clarity and hold all other copy and treatment variables fixed.
3. **The 13.5% target is not an expected outcome.** The approximate 3,000-per-
   arm count is a planning-only normal approximation; actual power requires a
   validated baseline, exclusions, attrition, and independent verification.
4. **The experiment was under-specified.** Randomization unit, cross-device
   assignment, cohort maturity, balance checks, guardrail thresholds, and
   stopping/peeking rules were missing. Pre-register these before launch.
5. **Synthetic feedback is circular.** DEMO-FACT-03 confirms the responses
   were authored to resemble the very hypotheses they appear to support. They
   cannot establish user need, prevalence, or demand.
6. **The illustrative economics can mislead.** DEMO-FACT-05 is a deterministic
   output, not a real LTV/CAC signal. Retention, margin, attribution, service
   costs, discounting, and cannibalization remain unverified or excluded.
7. **Candidate copy is a product promise.** “You control which files you
   share” needs product, access-control, privacy, and legal verification before
   any release.
8. **A recheck is only a trigger.** A new retention source must be assessed for
   provenance, period, cohort comparability, and method before the decision
   changes.

## Changes required before a real recommendation

- Reframe the immediate recommendation as **validate measurement first, then
  run a gated randomized permission-only test if the barrier is confirmed**.
- Mark the sample count as illustrative; require pre-registration and
  independent power verification using a validated eligible-user baseline.
- Keep synthetic feedback and fictional screens out of factual support for
  market/user claims; label them only as test-design prompts.
- Add acquisition mix, seasonality, tracking, and interval checks before
  attributing any decline to onboarding.
- Pre-register account-level randomization/deduplication, eligibility,
  exposure/balance checks, conversion maturity, guardrail thresholds, and
  stopping/interim-analysis policy.
- Do not release proposed messaging until product, privacy, billing, and legal
  owners validate the claims and exact terms.
- Make real LTV, CAC, 30-day retention, service costs, and cannibalization
  explicit gates for scaling or closure.

## Strategy revision

These objections are reflected in `artifacts/strategy.md`: the recommendation
is conditional, the experiment isolates permission clarity, the sample count
is a planning estimate subject to verification, synthetic materials are
explicitly non-evidence of demand, and experiment/copy/economics approval are
human-owned gates.
