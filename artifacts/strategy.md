# Family Cloud trial-to-paid strategy (fictional demonstration)

## Decision and evidence boundary

- [FACT] The synthetic funnel table lists Q1 conversion of 18% and Q4 conversion of 11%, a 7 percentage-point difference in the invented scenario. | evidence: DEMO-FACT-01 | confidence: high
- [FACT] The same synthetic table lists Q4 as 1,320 paid conversions from 12,000 eligible trials. | evidence: DEMO-FACT-02 | confidence: high
- [FACT] Feedback statements and onboarding descriptions are explicitly fictional and are not user research or observed interface evidence. | evidence: DEMO-FACT-03 | confidence: high
- [FACT] Eurostat's linked cloud-use dataset is labeled 2014–2020 and does not provide a directly comparable trial-to-paid benchmark for this case. | evidence: DEMO-FACT-04 | confidence: high
- [HYPOTHESIS] The apparent conversion decline may be caused by experience friction, offer/traffic changes, measurement changes, or a combination; the synthetic data cannot identify the cause. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-01 | confidence: low

## Options and recommendation

- [HYPOTHESIS] Keep-as-is is the safest immediate operating posture until real trial eligibility, events, and economics are validated. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-05 | confidence: medium
- [HYPOTHESIS] Closing the direction is not supported by this scenario because it contains no validated lifetime value, cannibalization, customer obligation, or real demand evidence. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-06 | confidence: medium
- [HYPOTHESIS] If a real owner approves a test, prioritize a single clarification of family-sharing permission boundaries while leaving storage-value copy, price, terms, eligibility, and acquisition mix unchanged. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] Do not launch Option D as a bundled production change until actual trial length, renewal price, cancellation route, and legal copy are verified. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-03 | confidence: low
- [FORECAST] The test is designed to detect whether a comprehension intervention can move paid conversion from the fictional Q4 baseline of 11% toward a 13.5% planning target; this is a target, not a forecast or expected real-world result. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-01 | confidence: low

## Product strategy and economics

- [FACT] Under the explicitly synthetic monthly assumptions, the deterministic calculator returns LTV 1,077.916667 RUB, CAC 1,000 RUB, and payback 7.730963 months before rounding. | evidence: DEMO-FACT-05 | confidence: high
- [HYPOTHESIS] This scenario LTV/CAC result is not decision-grade until monthly retention, gross margin, acquisition-spend attribution, paid-customer counts, servicing cost, and cannibalization are verified with authorized real data. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-04 | confidence: high
- [HYPOTHESIS] The proposed product direction is a bounded test of permission clarity only, not a commitment to add family features, change pricing, alter storage-value copy, or expand acquisition. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] For a real telecom/ecosystem product, include incremental ARPU, paid retention, support/network costs, attach-rate effects, and displacement of existing storage or partner offers in the next economic review. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-04 | confidence: high
- [FORECAST] The economic case should be rejected if verified incremental contribution after service costs and cannibalization is negative or if paid-conversion lift does not persist to the agreed retention checkpoint. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-02 | confidence: low

## Communication strategy and product truth

- [HYPOTHESIS] The fictional audience is an eligible mobile-service customer deciding whether family sharing is understandable and appropriate for their files. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] The candidate communication barrier is uncertainty about who can see shared files and what happens after a free trial. This is suggested only by invented material. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-03 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] A candidate message is “You control which files you share”; use it only if authoritative product and privacy evidence confirms that exact permission behavior. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] Use the trial-entry screen to explain the benefit and sharing boundary, the confirmation step to disclose verified duration/price/renewal/cancellation terms, and lifecycle messaging only to reinforce the same truthful value. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-03 | confidence: low

## Falsification and A/B validation plan

- [HYPOTHESIS] H1: among real eligible trial starters, uncertainty about family-sharing permissions materially contributes to abandonment or non-payment. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] H1 is weakened if real-user comprehension is already high and a randomized clarification treatment fails to improve paid conversion or activation versus control. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-02 | confidence: low
- [FORECAST] Proposed test design: randomize eligible trial-starting accounts 1:1 between the current screen and one permission-boundary clarification; leave price, trial terms, storage-value copy, eligibility, traffic mix, and other UI unchanged. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-03 | confidence: low
- [FORECAST] A two-sided alpha 0.05 and 80% power test for an illustrative change from 11.0% to 13.5% suggests roughly 2,700 users per arm by a two-proportion normal approximation; plan at least 3,000 only after a statistician verifies power, attrition, and a real baseline. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-03 | confidence: low
- [FORECAST] Pre-register a four-week enrollment window and wait for each eligible account's defined conversion window; allow a further 30 days to observe paid retention, refunds, and support guardrails. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-03 | confidence: low
- [FORECAST] Primary outcome: paid conversions divided by eligible randomized trial-starting accounts in the pre-defined window; guardrails: 30-day paid retention, refunds/cancellations, complaints, support contacts, gross contribution, and cannibalization. | evidence: DEMO-FORECAST-03 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] Before launch, define account-level assignment and deduplication, exclusions, exposure and balance checks, cross-device handling, guardrail thresholds, and a no-peeking analysis/stopping procedure; otherwise the experiment is not decision-grade. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-07 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] Stop or roll back if permission comprehension worsens, refunds or complaints exceed owner-approved thresholds, guardrails materially deteriorate, or the data-quality checks fail. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-07 | confidence: low
- [HYPOTHESIS] Keep-as-is remains the strongest counterargument until real, authorized research and validated instrumentation show a consequential, addressable barrier. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-05 | confidence: medium
- [HYPOTHESIS] A real experiment requires human approval, verified product and legal copy, experiment instrumentation, an owner, and an agreed guardrail threshold; this document authorizes no launch. | evidence: DEMO-HYP-05 | confidence: medium
