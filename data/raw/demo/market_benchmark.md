# Public cloud-use context — not a conversion benchmark

## Sources

- Eurostat, “Digital economy and society statistics — households and
  individuals,” extracted December 2025:
  <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Digital_economy_and_society_statistics_-_households_and_individuals>
- Eurostat dataset `isoc_cicci_use`, “Individuals — use of cloud services
  (2014–2020),” metadata updated 16 June 2024:
  <https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/api/dissemination/statistics/1.0/data/isoc_cicci_use?lang=en>

## What this can and cannot tell us

The article describes broad household and individual use of digital services
in the EU; the linked cloud-use dataset is explicitly limited to 2014–2020.
These sources establish a public, traceable context that cloud-service use has
been surveyed, but they do not supply a current, directly comparable
trial-to-paid conversion benchmark for this fictional mobile-operator product.
Country, year, survey question, population, product bundle, price, and funnel
denominator differ or are not specified for a like-for-like comparison.

Do not present EU cloud-service usage as Russian demand, paid conversion,
market size, willingness to pay, or product performance. The benchmark does
not validate the synthetic Q1–Q4 demo funnel. No comparable open
trial-conversion benchmark was identified for this exercise.
