# Demo project: Family Cloud

## Data provenance and scope

This is a fictional product case for demonstrating the workflow. It is not a
MegaFon product, is not based on MegaFon or other company data, and makes no
claim about real customers or market demand.

- `data/raw/demo/funnel_metrics.csv` is wholly synthetic, hand-authored
  illustrative cohort data.
- `data/raw/demo/user_feedback.md` contains fictional example statements, not
  public reviews, real interview transcripts, or recruited participants.
- `data/raw/demo/onboarding_screens.md` describes an invented screen flow; no
  actual product interface was inspected.
- `data/raw/demo/market_benchmark.md` cites the public Eurostat digital-economy
  article and an open Eurostat dataset. The Eurostat cloud-use dataset covers
  2014–2020, is old context, and is not comparable to this fictional product's
  trial conversion, subscription price, or retention. No market size or
  product benchmark is inferred from it.
- The economic input is an explicit scenario assumption, not an observed price,
  margin, CAC, or LTV.

The demonstration verifies evidence traceability, option comparison,
deterministic unit-economics calculations, independent critique, and
decision-recheck mechanics. Synthetic materials cannot establish actual
demand, diagnose a real conversion decline, or support a real launch decision.
