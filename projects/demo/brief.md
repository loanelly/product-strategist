# Project brief: Family Cloud trial conversion

## Decision

Should a fictional mobile operator test changes to a cloud-storage trial and
family-sharing onboarding, keep the current experience, or stop investing in
the direction?

## Context

The synthetic demonstration funnel shows trial-to-paid conversion declining
from 18% in Q1 to 11% in Q4. Every number is invented for this exercise. This
is not an observed MegaFon result and does not establish that conversion is
falling in any real product.

## Decision owner and audience

- **Decision owner:** fictional Head of Digital Products.
- **Answer audience:** product, growth, finance, customer-support, and
  communication leads.
- **Deadline:** choose whether to run a bounded validation test after reviewing
  the evidence and experiment guardrails.

## Goal and guardrails

- Determine which hypotheses could explain the illustrative funnel pattern.
- Recommend a reversible next step only if a real team can validate it.
- Primary demonstration metric: paid conversion among eligible trial starters.
- Guardrails for a real experiment: refunds, 30-day paid retention, complaints,
  support contacts, gross margin, and cannibalization of existing storage
  plans.
- Do not claim causation, actual customer need, or market demand from these
  synthetic inputs.

## Constraints and allowed data

Use only the synthetic demonstration files and the documented public Eurostat
context. No internal company data, private customer data, personal data, real
interviews, or unapproved third-party sources. The demo-screen description is
not a screenshot or observed interface.

## Approval checkpoint

This brief authorizes only analysis of this fictional demonstration. A real
product test, customer contact, product change, media spend, or external
commitment requires separate approval from the relevant human owner.
