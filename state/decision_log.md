This log contains one fictional demo decision and **no real product approval**.
For future entries, record a stable ID, approval date/context, selected and
rejected options, supporting evidence IDs, unresolved assumptions, owner, KPI,
deadline, and review conditions. Append corrections and superseding decisions
rather than erasing history.
